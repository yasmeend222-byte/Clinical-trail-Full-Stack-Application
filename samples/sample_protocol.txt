LIPID-GUARD: Evaluation of a PCSK9 inhibitor in high-risk hypertensive adults
Phase III, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled

Inclusion Criteria:
- Adults aged 40 to 80 years
- Diagnosis of hypertension
- LDL cholesterol >= 100 mg/dL despite stable therapy
- Currently taking a statin for at least 4 weeks
- Systolic blood pressure between 120 and 179 mmHg

Exclusion Criteria:
- History of stroke or myocardial infarction within the past 3 months
- eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73m2
- Active liver disease or ALT > 3 x ULN
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women
- Current treatment with an anticoagulant
- Participation in another clinical trial within 30 days
